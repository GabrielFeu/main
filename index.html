How I'd approach GTM at Fredrin

My first 30 days, as I'd plan them from the outside. I'd revise it after talking with the founders.

  • Instrument the funnel first.Wire download → sign-in → first ticket → first shipped change → team invite into one event model, connected to analytics, attribution and the CRM. You can't route or price what you can't see.
  • Route signups by product signal.Score new accounts on activation and team signals, enrich them automatically, and send team inquiries and high-intent workspaces to a founder's follow-up within minutes, not days.
  • Ship acquisition pages as experiments.Comparison and use-case pages for the AI coding tools developers already use, each with a measured conversion goal, shipped weekly.
  • Close the loop.A weekly written readout for the founders, covering what moved, what didn't and what we change next, so learning flows into positioning and pricing.
